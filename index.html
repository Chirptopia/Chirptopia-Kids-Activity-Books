Frequently Asked Questions

A few quick answers before you start.

Is the book educational or just for fun?

Both. Activities are designed to be playful while encouraging observation, thinking, creativity and early learning skills.

Can more than one child use it?

Yes. Shared activities work well for siblings, while coloring and writing pages are often best printed separately for each child.

Is this a treatment for screen addiction?

No. Chirptopia is an entertainment and learning product that provides offline alternatives; it is not a medical treatment.
